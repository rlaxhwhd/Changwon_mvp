## Actual-code corrections

- Archived professor records contain **7**, not 11, rows; all seven have no requestId or summary. `source='fixture'` is not a column of counsel_request/record: retain provenance in source_payload.
- `code_item` has a composite key. A single-column REFERENCES clause is invalid. Use the 018 generated-group + composite-FK pattern.
- `counsel.py.dto` calls strftime on slot_start/end whenever slot_date exists. Date-only professor records would crash request lists. Fix serialization and all slot consumers before enabling this path.
- ProfessorCounselRecords currently completes an existing request on the server, then appends a second local record. Preserve one request/one record, and replace this with **one** atomic server call for linked requests.
- `visibility()` returns false for assistants and grants professors records of other counselors through student scope. Neither is suitable for assistant aggregate reporting or the private professor record list. Use dedicated predicates.
- `student_access()` also permits historical request ownership. Direct recording must explicitly check current staff_student_scope; do not treat student_access alone as proof of current scope. Scope is the full E3 view (including department membership), not exclusively the advisor branch.
- notification has neither sender nor professor columns. Merely inserting tone/source_kind cannot reconstruct AdvisorNudge or restrict latest-nudge queries to the current professor/student pair.
- `counsel_record` is currently mutable via PUT and COMPLETE upserts. Protect completed PROF records from both paths. Existing non-PROF editing contracts stay intact.
- 037 emits notifications for REQUESTED and COMPLETE. Backfill must not generate seven historical request alerts or live completion alerts; direct recording must not notify its author of a new student application.
